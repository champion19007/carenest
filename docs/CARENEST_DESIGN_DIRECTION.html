CareNest · Implementation brief · 26 October 2026

One place for your family’s care.
Pets included.

A quieter, more useful CareNest: readable lists, a clear next action, and care options that work on a phone. Based on your Workspace reference and earlier blue-and-white medical app inspiration.

Design read: patient and family care discovery · calm and practical · Energy 2 / Rhythm 2 / Motion 1. This is a proposal preview; the example controls below demonstrate layout rather than submit real care requests.

What needs to change

Less repetition. Clearer decisions.

The homepage repeats doctors.Keep one useful provider list. Put clinic, video, home and pet care where people can find them before scrolling through profiles.
The empty appointment card dominates.Show a prominent appointment card only when an appointment exists. Use the space to help a new visitor choose care.
Every surface competes.Remove decorative gradients and floating boxes. Reserve cobalt for the main action and active selection. Keep status labels separate from brand colour.
Home care is incomplete.Add nurses, doctor/vet home visits, residential care and pet boarding. Capture an owned request with dates and track its review. Never imply an unconfirmed nurse or care-home bed is booked.
Small text and weak field borders.Use readable labels, visible keyboard focus and 44px controls. Keep list layouts usable at 320px, with no squeezed desktop tables.
Visual system

Neutral surfaces. One familiar blue.

White and cool grey in the day; charcoal and slate after dark. Colour helps you find the next action. It does not paint every icon, badge and heading.

Preview controls do not send messages, reserve slots or take payments.

Home care

Make the difference between a request and a booking obvious.

Nursing support

Choose a person or pet, city/PIN, preferred date and time window. Optional care notes stay encrypted. A team member reviews the request and records a response.

Doctor and vet visits

Link to doctors who offer home visits and retain the existing booking, address and clinic dispatch flow. If none are listed, let the patient submit a care request.

Residential stays

Include human care homes and pet boarding/assisted stays, with start and end dates. Availability, suitability and fees need review before confirmation.

A saved care request is not a confirmed appointment or emergency response. No invented partners, guaranteed callbacks, fake availability or unsupported prices. Existing demo appointments remain explicitly labelled.

Interaction & states

Usable when there is nothing to show, too.

No care requests yet

Your nursing, home-visit and residential-care requests will appear here. Start with who needs care and the service you need.

○ Awaiting review

Status always has a word, not only a colour. A request can be cancelled until it is closed.

Light, dark or system

Retain the visitor’s chosen theme, follow system changes when selected, and render the correct theme before first paint. Native form labels, error messages and keyboard focus remain visible.

Mobile filters use a modal dialog with Escape, focus containment and a clear close action.

Implementation & verification

Build the useful path all the way through.

  • Discovery homepage, provider cards, search filters, navigation and Home Care pages.
  • Signed-in request creation, patient history/cancellation and an MFA-protected admin review queue.
  • Additive migration, ownership checks, idempotency, encrypted notes, consent, rate limits and encryption-key rotation support.
  • Unit tests for request lifecycle and denied actions; production build and local browser checks in both themes and at phone/tablet/desktop widths.

Real service delivery still requires onboarded nurses, veterinarians and care-home/boarding partners. This redesign does not manufacture those relationships or claim provider approvals.
